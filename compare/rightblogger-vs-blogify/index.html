Goal: Generate a 1,600–2,000 word step-by-step tutorial ready for WordPress with zero manual prompt engineering.

Our Lab Observation on RightBlogger: RightBlogger's YouTube-to-Blog tool completely stripped out the YouTuber's 90-second sponsor read and channel intro. Because we selected the "Technical Tutorial" preset and enabled automatic internal linking, it formatted the DNS rules into clean copy-paste blocks and inserted 3 relevant internal links to existing WordPress articles automatically.

What Blogify.ai Produced (Time to Draft: 1 min 15 sec)

H1: Mastering WordPress Speed: Lowering TTFB Below 200ms Using Cloudflare TL;DR Summary: In this comprehensive guide based on our video walkthrough, we explore how Cloudflare Cache Rules dramatically improve WordPress Time to First Byte (TTFB) for global visitors. Our Lab Observation on Blogify.ai: Blogify finished 30 seconds faster and automatically generated a summary table plus pulled relevant snapshots. However, its default tone kept slightly more introductory filler ("In this comprehensive guide...") before hitting the technical configuration steps. Where Blogify shined was one-click syndication directly to Medium and LinkedIn right from the dashboard.
