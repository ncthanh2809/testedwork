About TestedWork & How We Benchmark Software

TestedWork.com is an independent software benchmarking publication focused on AI writing, SEO content optimization, video-to-blog repurposing, and digital publishing workflows.

Most software review sites simply rewrite a vendor's sales page. Our mission is to show creators, bloggers, and lean marketing teams what actually happens when you feed identical real-world inputs into competing SaaS tools.

1. How We Test & Calculate the TestedWork Lab Score

We clearly distinguish between hands-on workflow tests (where we run identical prompts, URLs, and keywords inside a live account and capture raw outputs) and specification evaluations (where we compare publicly documented pricing tiers, credit allowances, and CMS integrations).

Our 4-Part Lab Score Formula (10-Point Scale)

Every platform featured on our leaderboard is graded using the same weighted rubric:

Scoring Criterion Weight What We Measure
1. Output Structure & Accuracy 35% Heading hierarchy (H2/H3), factual accuracy, absence of generic AI filler, and formatting readiness.
2. Editing Time Saved 25% How many minutes of manual cleanup are required before an article or book chapter is ready to publish.
3. SEO, GEO & CMS Automation 20% Internal linking, NLP entity coverage, schema readiness, and direct 1-click publishing to WordPress, Medium, or KDP.
4. True Cost-Per-Asset Value 20% Real cost per published post or ebook at 20 vs. 60 outputs per month, including credit rollover rules and refund policies.

Strict Editorial Standards

  • Identical Inputs: When comparing two tools head-to-head, we use the exact same YouTube video URL, target keyword, or book outline prompt.
  • Balanced Pros, Cons & "Skip If" Guidance: No software is perfect for every user. Every review explicitly states who should not buy the tool and which alternative fits better.
  • No Paid Ratings: Software vendors cannot pay to alter our Lab Scores, test outputs, or comparison verdicts.
